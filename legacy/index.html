RPG Birmingham

Birmingham has things that need doing. Go do one.

Local nonprofits post small volunteer jobs: an hour in a teaching garden, a shift sorting food. Pick one on the map, show up, and earn credits you can spend at local shops or give to a cause.

  1. PickFind a quest on the map. Filter by the group running it.
  2. GoAccept it and we open directions in Google Maps.
  3. Check inMeet the host, get their 4-digit code, collect credits and XP.

Quests from Birmingham Promise, Firehouse Ministries, Jones Valley Teaching Farm and Ed Farm.
